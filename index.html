Questions

The honest FAQ

Do I need to bring my own API keys?+

No. Market data and AI coaching run through keys we host server-side — you never configure or see a key. You just sign in and use the desk.

How is this different from a normal backtester?+

Two things. First, it's structurally leak-proof: higher timeframes are aggregated only from bars already revealed, so the outcome can't leak into your decision. Second, the AI layer is barred from producing numbers — it explains the figures your code computed and coaches process, so it can't hallucinate an edge that isn't there.

Is the AI giving me trade signals?+

Never. The Professor has no view on where price goes and says so if asked. It assesses whether you followed your rules, sized correctly, and managed the trade the way you said you would. It's a process coach, not a signal service.

What markets are covered?+

Real CME/CBOT/NYMEX/COMEX futures (continuous front-month) via Databento, plus major FX pairs and BTC/ETH via Alpha Vantage. Anything else, drop a CSV export and the same engines run on it.

Is this financial advice?+

No. ProFX is educational analysis of your own recorded behavior and historical data. It gives no investment, tax, or legal advice, and makes no guarantee of profitability.
